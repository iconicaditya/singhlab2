	Singh Lab | Environment
Research, education, and community action for a sustainable future.
The Singh Lab is an interdisciplinary research group at Kobe City University of Foreign Studies focused on understanding and addressing environmental challenges through applied research, education, and community engagement.
	Research Areas:
Plastics · Waste Management · Climate Change · Sustainable Tourism · Community Sustainability
	About the Lab
The Singh Lab conducts interdisciplinary environmental research addressing pressing global and local challenges. Our work spans plastics pollution, waste management systems, climate change adaptation, and sustainable tourism, with a strong emphasis on community engagement and policy relevance.
We combine academic research with real-world action, working closely with communities, students, policymakers, and civil society to develop practical solutions for sustainability.
i) Vision
To integrate research, teaching, and local action to reduce environmental risks and improve environmental and social well-being.
Ii) Mission
Produce high-quality, policy-relevant environmental research
Train students in applied and field-based research methods
Support evidence-based environmental decision-making
Engage communities in sustainability initiatives
Contribute to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)
iii) Our Approach
Our research is grounded in real-world contexts and emphasizes impact beyond academia.
Mixed Methods: Quantitative data, qualitative interviews, and participatory research
Field-First Research: Direct engagement with communities and environments
Policy Relevance: Research designed to inform local and regional policy
Interdisciplinary Perspective: Linking environmental, social, and economic dimensions
SDG Alignment: Explicit contribution to global sustainability goals

	Research Areas
i. Plastics
We study the production, use, and disposal of plastics, with a focus on reducing environmental leakage and promoting circular economy solutions.
Key topics include:
Marine and coastal plastic pollution
Single-use plastics reduction
Plastic waste governance
Community-based monitoring and education
ii. Waste Management
Our work examines waste systems from household to municipal levels, identifying opportunities for improved efficiency and sustainability.
Key topics include:
Urban waste systems
Recycling and composting
Campus waste management
Behavioral change and waste reduction policies
iii. Climate Change
We focus on local-scale climate impacts and community-based responses to climate risks.
Key topics include:
Climate adaptation and resilience
Risk perception and communication
Urban and community-level climate strategies
Education and awareness initiatives

iv. Sustainable Tourism
We explore pathways for tourism that support environmental protection and community well-being.
Key topics include:
Environmental impacts of tourism
Community-based tourism models
Sustainable tourism policy
Destination management and planning
